The paper trail

How a file moves through the cell

01

Register & verify eligibility

Create your student file with academic records. The cell checks CGPA and backlog eligibility against each drive automatically.

02

Apply to open roles

Submit one application per role from your dashboard. Your resume and transcript are attached from your profile.

03

Sit for the drive

Shortlists, test slots and interview rounds are posted to your notifications as the recruiter updates them.

04

Offer, accept, close the file

Accepted offers are logged against your file and the placement cell handles documentation from there.
