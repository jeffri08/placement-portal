Frequently asked

Who can register on this portal?+

Any currently enrolled student in their pre-final or final year, once the placement cell has activated the batch for the season.

How is eligibility for a role decided?+

Each posted role lists a minimum CGPA, branch and backlog condition. The portal checks your profile against those automatically when you open an application.

Can I apply to more than one company at a time?+

Yes, there's no cap on applications. Some recruiters do restrict students who've already accepted an offer from applying further, which is noted on the relevant drive.

What happens after I accept an offer?+

Your file is marked placed for the season and the cell begins document verification with the company directly. You can still view but not apply to further roles.

How do I update my resume for one application only?+

Resumes are attached from your profile at the moment you apply, so update your profile first if a specific application needs a different version.

Who do I contact if a drive date changes?+

Drive changes are posted to the calendar and your notifications first. For anything urgent, reach the desk listed below.
