About the cell

The office between your degree and your first offer letter.

The Ridgeview Placement Cell coordinates every recruiter visit, eligibility check and offer letter on campus — and this portal is the file room where that work happens.

What we do

We invite recruiters to campus, verify student eligibility against each role, run the test and interview logistics, and keep an official record of every offer made. Every action in this portal writes back to that record.

Who runs it

A faculty placement coordinator, three student placement representatives per department, and a recruiter relations desk that maintains company files year-round.
